Case 1 — Safety first

Recap — three patterns to carry into the exam

  • Evaluate before intervene. When one option is an evaluation and the rest are interventions, the evaluation is usually the initial step — unless safety demands immediate action.
  • The client-stated goal leads. Family input informs the plan; the client's priority orders it.
  • New symptoms → stop, assess, report. New or worsening symptoms outrank the treatment plan; answer "stop, assess, report" before "adapt" or "defer".
