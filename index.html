OTD 6185 Scholarly Practice III

Interactive activity previews for faculty review

How to review: open each activity and try it as a student would. Then choose Use it, Don't use it, or Request a change under it, here or at the bottom of the activity, and add a note if you like. When you're done, select Copy my choices and paste them into an email to keith_pace@baylor.edu. Your choices are saved in this browser as you go. These previews don't record anything about how you do in the activities.
Module 1

What pattern is emerging?

Drag and Drop

Sort 9 short student baseline snapshots into the 3 patterns from the Module 1 lesson: perception and performance align, they don't align, or content may not tell the whole story.

Outcome: Compare perceived readiness with demonstrated performance to identify individualized priorities for NBCOT preparation (Module 1).

Open activity
Module 2

Identify and filter: what matters for this decision?

Mark the Words

In 3 NBCOT-style stems, mark the directive word and the details that change the answer (8 in all), leaving nonessential details unmarked.

Outcome: Identify key terminology and cues within NBCOT-style questions and differentiate relevant information from distractors (Module 2).

Open activity
Module 3

Two good answers: eliminate, choose, explain

Branching Scenario

Work 3 cases with 3 decisions each: what to eliminate first, the best response, and which of the 5 considerations (safety, client priorities, occupational performance, OT process, context) decided it.

Outcome: Differentiate between plausible and most appropriate responses, and prioritize safety, client-centeredness, level of care and occupational performance (Module 3).

Open activity
Module 4

Which pathway, and which strategy first?

Dropdown

For 8 student practice notes, choose the pathway (A–D) and the first strategy to try from the pathway lessons (16 dropdowns).

Outcome: Interpret baseline data to identify individual test-performance vulnerabilities and apply targeted strategies (Module 4).

Open activity
Module 5

What type of error was it?

Drag and Drop

Sort 12 missed-question notes into the 4 error categories from the Module 5 lesson: knowledge gap, question interpretation, clinical reasoning and test-taking behavior.

Outcome: Categorize errors made during NBCOT-style practice questions and differentiate knowledge deficits from test-taking and clinical-reasoning errors (Module 5).

Open activity
Module 6

Outside prep resource hub

Interactive Map

Open 8 prep resources (TrueLearn, NBCOT tools and others) to see what each is for and when it fits in a study plan.

Outcome: Select strategies and resources appropriate for continued NBCOT preparation (Module 6).

Open activity
Module 7

Then vs. now: what has changed?

Guided Documentation

Enter baseline and final results, answer reflection prompts, and complete a readiness checklist.

Outcome: Evaluate changes from baseline to completion and identify remaining priorities (Module 7).
