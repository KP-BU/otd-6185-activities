Which pathway does this test-taker need?

Each vignette describes a real pattern from practice-question data. Choose the pathway that fits, then the first strategy you'd try. Check when all blanks are filled.

Vignette 1

Jordan's accuracy is 72% for the first 40 questions of every block and 55% for the last 40. He rarely flags questions and doesn't change answers. He finishes with time to spare.

Vignette 2

Priya's overall accuracy is 68%, but her change log shows 11 answers changed per block, 8 of them from right to wrong. She describes "a voice saying the obvious answer can't be right."

Vignette 3

Marcus scores 74% on untimed practice and 58% on timed mocks. He reports his heart racing before each block and "blanking" on the first few questions.

Vignette 4

Elena has read the review book twice and highlights heavily, but her content-area scores haven't moved in three cycles and she can't say which areas are weakest.

Vignette 5

Sam averages 84 seconds per question, leaves 6–8 items unanswered when time runs out, and his accuracy on answered items is strong at 71%.

Vignette 6

After a difficult question, Dana's next three or four answers are usually wrong even when the content is easy; her overall score recovers later in the block.
