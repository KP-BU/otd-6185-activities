Module 2 · Anatomy of an NBCOT Question

Find the cues, spot the distractors

In each stem, tap the words that tell you what the question is really asking. Cue words set the priority; distractor phrases are there to pull you off it.

Passage 1 — mark the cue words

Tap every cue word — the words that tell you what kind of answer the question wants (priority, sequence, timing). Then choose Check.

Passage 2 — mark cues and distractors

Pick a mode, then tap. Mark the cues that should drive your answer, and switch to Distractor mode to mark the phrases that are true but pull you off the clinical picture. Tapping a marked word again clears it.

Cue vocabulary — keep this in view while you read a stem

Priority / selection cues: BEST, MOST, FIRST, NEXT, PRIORITY, INITIAL, SAFEST.

Timing cues: days or weeks post-op, stage of recovery or condition, age of the client — these set which precautions and expectations apply right now.
