Before you start

Have three things open: your Module 1 Baseline NBCOT Readiness Profile, your final TrueLearn mock exam report, and your final NBCOT Entry-Level OTR Self-Assessment summary. You'll retake the Test Performance Self-Assessment on this page.

Section 1 — TrueLearn mock exam: baseline vs final

Baseline numbers are on your Module 1 Baseline NBCOT Readiness Profile (questions 1–11). Final numbers come from your final TrueLearn mock exam. Every field is required.

Section 2 — NBCOT Self-Assessment: baseline vs final

Use the Summary Report from each NBCOT Entry-Level OTR Self-Assessment. Baseline counts are questions 13–16 on your readiness profile.

Section 3 — Test Performance Self-Assessment

Step 1. Enter your six baseline domain scores (questions 20–25 on your readiness profile; each is out of 20).

Step 2. Retake the self-assessment. Think about your typical experience on exams and practice exams since Module 1. For each statement, choose how often it happens: 0 = Never, 1 = Rarely, 2 = Sometimes, 3 = Often, 4 = Almost Always. Your final domain scores are added up for you.

Higher scores mean a greater potential need for support in that area. Score ranges are preliminary course guidelines, not validated diagnostic cut scores.

Section 4 — What changed

Computed for you from Sections 1–3. Change = final − baseline. Percent change = (final − baseline) ÷ baseline × 100. Rows fill in as you enter valid numbers.
